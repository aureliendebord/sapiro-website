The smallest country in Europe is Vatican City, at just [0.44 km²](https://www.vaticanstate.va/en/state-and-government/general-informations/geography.html). Europe packs an unusual number of tiny states: five of the ten smallest countries in the world are here. Below is the ranking of the 10 smallest European countries by area, from the Vatican to Montenegro.

## 1. Vatican City (0.44 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/va.svg" alt="Flag of Vatican City" loading="lazy" />


The smallest country in Europe and in the world. [882 residents at the end of 2024](https://www.vaticanstate.va/en/state-and-government/general-informations/population.html), enclaved within Rome, ruled by the Pope. An elective theocracy with its own post office and the Swiss Guard for an army.

## 2. Monaco (2 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/mc.svg" alt="Flag of Monaco" loading="lazy" />


A Mediterranean principality of roughly 39,000 people, ruled by the Grimaldi family since 1297. The most densely populated country on Earth, famous for its Formula 1 Grand Prix and the Monte-Carlo casino.

## 3. San Marino (61 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/sm.svg" alt="Flag of San Marino" loading="lazy" />


Landlocked inside Italy, San Marino claims to be the world's oldest surviving republic, founded in 301. About 34,000 inhabitants perched around Monte Titano.

## 4. Liechtenstein (160 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/li.svg" alt="Flag of Liechtenstein" loading="lazy" />


A doubly landlocked Alpine principality between Switzerland and Austria, with around 40,000 people. Wealthy and German-speaking.

## 5. Malta (315 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/mt.svg" alt="Flag of Malta" loading="lazy" />


An archipelago south of Sicily and the smallest member state of the European Union. Around 570,000 people and a history running from the Knights of St John to British rule.

## 6. Andorra (468 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/ad.svg" alt="Flag of Andorra" loading="lazy" />


A Pyrenean co-principality between France and Spain, jointly headed by the French president and the Bishop of Urgell. Roughly 82,000 residents, no airport, and a tourism-and-shopping economy.

## 7. Luxembourg (2,586 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/lu.svg" alt="Flag of Luxembourg" loading="lazy" />


A landlocked grand duchy and one of the founding members of the European Union. Around 680,000 people, three official languages, and one of the highest GDP-per-capita figures in the world.

## 8. Cyprus (9,251 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/cy.svg" alt="Flag of Cyprus" loading="lazy" />


An eastern Mediterranean island, an EU member since 2004, divided since 1974 between the Republic of Cyprus and the self-declared north. About 1.4 million inhabitants on the island as a whole.

## 9. Kosovo (10,905 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/xk.svg" alt="Flag of Kosovo" loading="lazy" />


Europe's youngest country, having declared independence from Serbia in 2008. Around 1.6 million people on [10,905 km² according to Eurostat](https://db.nomics.world/Eurostat/cpc_agmain/A.1601.XK); its statehood is recognised by many countries but not all.

## 10. Montenegro (13,888 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/me.svg" alt="Flag of Montenegro" loading="lazy" />


A mountainous Adriatic country of roughly 620,000 people, independent from Serbia since 2006 and a candidate for European Union membership.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the smallest country in Europe?

Vatican City is the smallest country in Europe, at 0.44 km². It is also the smallest country in the world, enclaved within the city of Rome.

### Is Monaco or the Vatican smaller?

The Vatican (0.44 km²) is smaller than Monaco (2 km²). Monaco is the second-smallest country in Europe and the most densely populated country in the world.

### What is the smallest country in the European Union?

Malta is the smallest EU member state by area, at 315 km². The Vatican, Monaco, San Marino, Liechtenstein and Andorra are smaller but are not members of the European Union.

### What are the European microstates?

The European microstates are the six smallest sovereign states of the continent: Vatican City, Monaco, San Marino, Liechtenstein, Andorra and Malta. They combine tiny territory with full independence.
