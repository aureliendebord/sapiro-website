## Geography

**1. Which is the only country in the world whose flag is not rectangular?**
**Nepal.** Its flag is made of two stacked triangles, often linked to the Himalayas and to the country's two main religions.

**2. What is the capital of Kazakhstan?**
**Astana.** The city was called Nur-Sultan between 2019 and 2022, after the country's first president, before getting its name back.

**3. Which two South American countries are landlocked?**
**Bolivia and Paraguay.** Bolivia lost its coastline to Chile in the War of the Pacific, at the end of the 19th century, and still claims it.

**4. What is the deepest lake in the world?**
**Lake Baikal**, in Siberia. It is more than 1,600 meters deep and holds about a fifth of the planet's unfrozen surface fresh water.

**5. In which country is most of the Atacama Desert?**
**Chile.** It is the driest non-polar desert in the world: some areas have gone decades without recorded rainfall.

**6. Which European river flows through four capital cities?**
**The Danube:** Vienna, Bratislava, Budapest and Belgrade. No other river in the world runs through as many capitals.

## History

**7. How long did the Hundred Years' War actually last?**
**116 years**, from 1337 to 1453, with long truces. The name was given long after it ended.

**8. In what year did Constantinople fall to the Ottomans?**
**In 1453**, the same year the Hundred Years' War ended. Conquered by Mehmed II, the city became the capital of the Ottoman Empire.

**9. Which 1494 treaty divided the New World between Spain and Portugal?**
**The Treaty of Tordesillas.** It drew a line west of the Cape Verde islands: everything to the east went to Portugal, which is why Brazil speaks Portuguese.

**10. Which country was the first to give women the vote in national elections?**
**New Zealand, in 1893.** In the United Kingdom, some women won the vote in 1918, but equal voting rights with men only came in 1928.

**11. What was the capital of the Inca Empire?**
**Cusco**, in present-day Peru. Machu Picchu, far more famous today, was a royal estate, not the capital.

**12. How many wives did Henry VIII of England have?**
**Six.** Two of them, Anne Boleyn and Catherine Howard, were beheaded. The first, Catherine of Aragon, was the daughter of Spain's Catholic Monarchs.

## Art and culture

**13. Who painted "The Garden of Earthly Delights"?**
**Hieronymus Bosch**, a Netherlandish painter of the late 15th century. The triptych hangs in the Prado Museum in Madrid.

**14. In which city is the Hermitage Museum?**
**Saint Petersburg.** The museum fills several buildings, including the tsars' Winter Palace.

**15. Which composer wrote the "Boléro"?**
**Maurice Ravel**, in 1928. Originally a ballet score, it became one of the most performed pieces of classical music.

**16. Which architect designed the Guggenheim Museum in Bilbao?**
**Frank Gehry.** Opened in 1997, its curving titanium-clad facade changed the image of the city.

**17. Who wrote the play "Waiting for Godot"?**
**Samuel Beckett**, an Irish writer who first wrote it in French. It premiered in Paris in 1953.

**18. In which museum is Picasso's "Guernica" displayed?**
**The Museo Reina Sofía in Madrid.** The painting only arrived in Spain in 1981, after decades at the Museum of Modern Art in New York.

## Science

**19. What is the chemical symbol for tungsten?**
**W.** It comes from its German name, *Wolfram*. The element was first isolated in 1783 by two Spanish chemists, the Elhuyar brothers.

**20. Which planet has a day longer than its year?**
**Venus.** It takes about 243 Earth days to spin once on its axis and about 225 to orbit the Sun.

**21. What is the longest bone in the human body?**
**The femur**, the thigh bone. It makes up roughly a quarter of a person's height.

**22. At what temperature do the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales show the same number?**
**−40 degrees.** It is the only point where the two scales meet.

**23. Which blood type is known as the universal donor?**
**O negative.** It can be given to patients of any blood group in an emergency, which is why hospitals keep it in stock.

**24. Which particle in an atom has no electric charge?**
**The neutron.** Together with protons, it forms the nucleus; electrons orbit around it.

## Nature

**25. Which is the only mammal capable of true flight?**
**The bat.** Other mammals, such as flying squirrels, can only glide.

**26. How many bones are in a shark's skeleton?**
**None.** A shark's skeleton is made entirely of cartilage, the same flexible tissue as in the human nose and ears.

**27. Which tree species is the tallest in the world?**
**The coast redwood** (*Sequoia sempervirens*), from California. The tallest known specimen, named Hyperion, is about 116 meters tall.

**28. Which animal has fingerprints almost identical to human ones?**
**The koala.** Its prints look so much like ours that they are very hard to tell apart, even under a microscope.

**29. Which bird can fly backwards?**
**The hummingbird.** It beats its wings so fast that it can hover in place and move backwards.

**30. What color is a polar bear's skin, under its fur?**
**Black.** Its hair is not actually white but translucent: it reflects light, while the dark skin absorbs the sun's heat.

## Frequently asked questions

### What makes a general knowledge question hard?

A clear answer that few people know: a precise fact (a date, a name, a number) or an idea that goes against what most people believe, such as the color of a polar bear's skin.
