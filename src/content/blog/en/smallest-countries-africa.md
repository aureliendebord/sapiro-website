The smallest country in Africa is the Seychelles, at 457 km² according to the [UN Demographic Yearbook 2025](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/documents/dyb2025/table03.pdf), slightly more than the city of Vienna. The continent of giants like Algeria also holds a string of micro-states, nearly all of them islands. On the mainland itself, the smallest is the Gambia. Here is the ranking of the ten smallest African countries.

## 1. Seychelles (457 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/sc.svg" alt="Flag of Seychelles" loading="lazy" />


An archipelago of 115 islands in the Indian Ocean, home to about 120,000 people in 2024 according to the [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL), the least populated country in Africa. Its economy runs on tourism and fishing, and its maritime domain is huge: [about 1.4 million km² of exclusive economic zone](https://www.adaptation-undp.org/explore/africa/seychelles), nearly 3,000 times its land area.

## 2. São Tomé and Príncipe (964 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/st.svg" alt="Flag of São Tomé and Príncipe" loading="lazy" />


Two volcanic islands in the Gulf of Guinea, a former Portuguese colony independent since 1975. In the early 20th century the islands ranked among the world's very largest cocoa producers, and the country still lives partly off it.

## 3. Comoros (1,861 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/km.svg" alt="Flag of Comoros" loading="lazy" />


An archipelago between Madagascar and Mozambique. The union claims a fourth island, Mayotte, which remained French after the 1974 referendum, one of the continent's most durable territorial disputes. The UN area figure does not include Mayotte.

## 4. Mauritius (1,979 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/mu.svg" alt="Flag of Mauritius" loading="lazy" />


Mauritius is one of Africa's most developed economies, driven by tourism, finance and textiles. It was also the only home of the dodo, extinct since the 17th century. The UN area figure leaves out the outlying islands of Agalega and St. Brandon.

## 5. Cape Verde (4,033 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/cv.svg" alt="Flag of Cape Verde" loading="lazy" />


Ten volcanic islands off the coast of Senegal. With no notable natural resources, the country relies on services and tourism, and its diaspora far outnumbers its resident population.

## 6. The Gambia (11,295 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/gm.svg" alt="Flag of The Gambia" loading="lazy" />


The smallest country on the African mainland. The Gambia is a strip of land [25 to 50 km wide](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/aquastat/pdf_files/GMB_gambia_cp.pdf) that follows the river of the same name, entirely surrounded by Senegal except for its Atlantic coastline.

## 7. Eswatini (17,363 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/sz.svg" alt="Flag of Eswatini" loading="lazy" />


The former Swaziland, renamed in 2018, is one of the world's last absolute monarchies. The kingdom sits between South Africa and Mozambique.

## 8. Djibouti (23,200 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/dj.svg" alt="Flag of Djibouti" loading="lazy" />


Small in size, strategic in position: the country guards the Bab-el-Mandeb strait, the gateway between the Red Sea and the Indian Ocean, and hosts military bases of several powers.

## 9. Rwanda (26,338 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/rw.svg" alt="Flag of Rwanda" loading="lazy" />


The land of a thousand hills is one of Africa's most densely populated countries, with about 14 million inhabitants in 2024 on an area comparable to Belgium.

## 10. Burundi (27,834 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/bi.svg" alt="Flag of Burundi" loading="lazy" />


Rwanda's neighbor and near geographic twin, Burundi closes this ranking. Rwanda is bordered to the west by [Lake Kivu](https://www.gov.rw/about); Burundi, for its part, lies on [Lake Tanganyika, the second-deepest lake in the world](https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/Earth_from_Space_Africa_s_ancient_Lake_Tanganyika).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the smallest country in Africa?

The Seychelles, at 457 km² spread over 115 islands in the Indian Ocean. It is also the least populated country on the continent, with about 120,000 inhabitants in 2024.

### What is the smallest country on the African mainland?

The Gambia, at 11,295 km². This strip of land follows the Gambia River for about 300 km and is surrounded by Senegal, except for its short Atlantic coastline.

### Is the Seychelles smaller than Vatican City?

No, not even close. [Vatican City covers 0.44 km²](https://www.vaticanstate.va/en/state-and-government/general-informations/geography.html): the Seychelles is about 1,000 times larger. But at the scale of Africa, whose area exceeds 30 million km², it remains tiny.

### Why are Africa's smallest countries islands?

Africa's mainland borders, inherited from colonization, carve out large blocks. The archipelagos, colonized and decolonized separately, became full states despite their small size.
