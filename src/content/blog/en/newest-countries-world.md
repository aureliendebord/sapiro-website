The newest country in the world is South Sudan, independent since July 9, 2011. The political map kept shifting long after the Cold War ended. Since 1990, dozens of states have appeared, usually through a referendum or the breakup of a federation. The ranking below starts with the most recent.

## 1. South Sudan (2011)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/ss.svg" alt="Flag of South Sudan" loading="lazy" />


Independent from Sudan after a [referendum approved by 98.83% of voters](https://peacekeeping.un.org/sites/default/files/past/unmis/referendum.shtml) in January 2011. It became the 193rd [member state of the UN](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/member-states) on July 14, 2011. Its birth ended decades of civil war between northern and southern Sudan.

## 2. Kosovo (2008)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/xk.svg" alt="Flag of Kosovo" loading="lazy" />


Declared independence from Serbia in February 2008. It is recognized by around a hundred countries ([108 recognitions](https://mfa-ks.net/lista-e-njohjeve/) listed by its foreign ministry as of November 2026), but is not a UN member. Serbia and several major powers, including Russia and China, do not recognize it.

## 3. Montenegro (2006)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/me.svg" alt="Flag of Montenegro" loading="lazy" />


Separated peacefully from Serbia after a [referendum won with 55.5% of the vote](https://www.britannica.com/place/Montenegro) on May 21, 2006, and a declaration of independence on June 3. This small Balkan country on the Adriatic joined the UN the same year.

## 4. Serbia (2006)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/rs.svg" alt="Flag of Serbia" loading="lazy" />


Technically a new state: Serbia became an independent country upon the dissolution of the union of Serbia and Montenegro. It is, however, considered the continuator state of that union, whose UN seat it kept.

## 5. East Timor (2002)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/tl.svg" alt="Flag of East Timor" loading="lazy" />


A former Portuguese colony, the territory went through Indonesian occupation and then a transitional UN administration that led to independence. Fully sovereign since 2002, it is the youngest state in Asia.

## 6. Palau (1994)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/pw.svg" alt="Flag of Palau" loading="lazy" />


This Pacific archipelago of about 18,000 inhabitants became independent on October 1, 1994, at the end of the American trusteeship. It remains linked to the United States through a Compact of Free Association.

## 7. Eritrea (1993)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/er.svg" alt="Flag of Eritrea" loading="lazy" />


After three decades of war for independence, Eritrea seceded from Ethiopia. The UN-supervised referendum of April 1993 was approved by an overwhelming majority.

## 8. Czechia (1993)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/cz.svg" alt="Flag of Czechia" loading="lazy" />


Born from the "Velvet Divorce": Czechoslovakia split into two states on January 1, 1993, without a single shot fired. Czechia [kept the Czechoslovak flag](https://www.britannica.com/topic/flag-of-the-Czech-Republic).

## 9. Slovakia (1993)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/sk.svg" alt="Flag of Slovakia" loading="lazy" />


The other half of the Velvet Divorce, independent on the same day as Czechia. It adopted a new flag and joined the UN on January 19, 1993.

## 10. Bosnia and Herzegovina (1992)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/ba.svg" alt="Flag of Bosnia and Herzegovina" loading="lazy" />


The country became independent during the breakup of Yugoslavia, after a referendum in 1992. A war followed and lasted until the Dayton Agreement in 1995.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the newest country in the world?

South Sudan is the newest country in the world, independent since July 9, 2011. It separated from Sudan after a referendum approved by 98.83% of voters and became the 193rd member state of the UN.

### What could be the next country in the world?

Bougainville is the most likely candidate. This island in Papua New Guinea voted 97.7% in favor of independence in a non-binding 2019 referendum. Papua New Guinea's parliament still has to ratify the result: the [vote is scheduled for December 2, 2026](https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/pacific/2825651/png-parliament-nears-crucial-vote-on-bougainville-s-future) and requires a three-quarters majority.

### How many countries are there in the world?

The UN has 193 member states, plus 2 observer states: the Vatican and Palestine. The total varies depending on whether partially recognized states like Kosovo or Taiwan are included.

### Why did South Sudan separate from Sudan?

The separation ended decades of civil war between northern Sudan, predominantly Arab and Muslim, and the south, predominantly Christian and animist. The 2005 peace agreement provided for a self-determination referendum, held in 2011 and approved by 98.83%.
