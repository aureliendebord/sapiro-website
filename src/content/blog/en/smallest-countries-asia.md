The smallest country in Asia is the Maldives, at about 300 km² according to the [UN Demographic Yearbook 2025](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/documents/dyb2025/table03.pdf). Asia is the largest continent, yet it holds some of the world's tiniest nations: scattered islands and a couple of city-states. Here is the ranking of the 10 smallest Asian countries by area.

## 1. Maldives (300 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/mv.svg" alt="Flag of Maldives" loading="lazy" />


An archipelago of [around 1,190 coral islands](https://www.adaptation-undp.org/explore/asia-and-pacific/maldives) in the Indian Ocean, home to roughly 530,000 people. The lowest-lying country on Earth: more than 80% of its land lies less than one metre above sea level.

## 2. Singapore (736 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/sg.svg" alt="Flag of Singapore" loading="lazy" />


A city-state at the tip of the Malay Peninsula, with around 6 million people. One of the world's leading financial and shipping hubs, and among the most densely populated countries.

## 3. Bahrain (778 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/bh.svg" alt="Flag of Bahrain" loading="lazy" />


An island kingdom in the Persian Gulf, connected to Saudi Arabia by a causeway. Around 1.6 million inhabitants and an economy built on oil, finance and aluminium.

## 4. Brunei (5,765 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/bn.svg" alt="Flag of Brunei" loading="lazy" />


A small, oil-rich sultanate on the island of Borneo, surrounded by Malaysia. Around 460,000 people and one of the highest living standards in Southeast Asia.

## 5. Palestine (6,020 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/ps.svg" alt="Flag of Palestine" loading="lazy" />


Comprising the West Bank and the Gaza Strip, with a combined population of roughly 5.3 million. Recognised as a state by a majority of UN members.

## 6. Cyprus (9,251 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/cy.svg" alt="Flag of Cyprus" loading="lazy" />


Geographically in West Asia, this eastern Mediterranean island is a member of the European Union. About 1.4 million inhabitants on the island as a whole, divided since 1974.

## 7. Lebanon (10,452 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/lb.svg" alt="Flag of Lebanon" loading="lazy" />


A mountainous country on the eastern Mediterranean, around 5.8 million people, with a long history as a crossroads of Phoenician, Roman, Arab and Ottoman cultures.

## 8. Qatar (11,682 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/qa.svg" alt="Flag of Qatar" loading="lazy" />


A gas-rich peninsula on the Persian Gulf, around 2.9 million residents (mostly expatriates). Host of the 2022 FIFA World Cup.

## 9. East Timor (14,954 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/tl.svg" alt="Flag of East Timor" loading="lazy" />


Timor-Leste, on the eastern half of the island of Timor, independent since 2002. Around 1.4 million people; one of Asia's youngest nations.

## 10. Kuwait (17,818 km²)
<img class="flag-badge" src="/flags/countries/kw.svg" alt="Flag of Kuwait" loading="lazy" />


A small, oil-rich state at the head of the Persian Gulf, around 4.9 million residents. Among the wealthiest countries per capita in the region.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the smallest country in Asia?

The Maldives is the smallest country in Asia by area, at about 300 km². It is an archipelago of roughly 1,190 coral islands in the Indian Ocean.

### Is Singapore or the Maldives smaller?

The Maldives (300 km²) is smaller than Singapore (736 km²) by area. Singapore, however, has a far larger population, close to six million against about half a million.

### What is the smallest country in the Middle East?

Bahrain is the smallest country in the Middle East by area, at about 778 km². It is an island kingdom in the Persian Gulf, linked to Saudi Arabia by a causeway.

### Which is the lowest-lying country in Asia?

The Maldives is the lowest-lying country in Asia and in the world, with more than 80% of its land less than one metre above sea level, which makes it especially exposed to rising seas.
